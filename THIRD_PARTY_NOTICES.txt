Xenia Xbox 360 Emulator Research Project
https://github.com/xenia-project/xenia

XEX2/STFS structure definitions and STFS block-address / active hash-table
selection algorithms in rghfps/formats.py follow the published Xenia sources.
The GOD/SVOD sector mapping in rghfps/god.py also follows Xenia's published
reader. Its hash-chain layout was cross-checked against the iso2god-rs writer:
https://github.com/iliazeus/iso2god-rs/tree/master/src/god

The offline catalog contains title/build names, Title IDs and source links from
xenia-canary/game-patches at fe957a050e37f5a1e3f485ca43c2e37ee2a9c92d.
It does not distribute those patches or imply that they are console compatible.

XexTool is not included. Its executable, dependencies and licenses remain
separate from this workbench. No Xbox game executable or game asset is shipped.

XBDM wire-protocol behavior was cross-checked against public implementations:
https://github.com/Experiment5X/XBDM
https://github.com/MobCat/xbdm.py
The Python client here is a project implementation; no Microsoft SDK or XBDM
plugin binary is included.
